# 1. Purpose

This document defines the permanent principles that guide every design,
architectural and implementation decision within the PersonaDNA platform.

Whenever multiple technical solutions exist, the solution that best
satisfies these principles shall be preferred.

---

# 2. Principle 1 — Understanding Before Generation

Understanding always comes before generation.

PersonaDNA shall understand context, identity, intent and meaning before
attempting to generate responses, recommendations or content.

---

# 3. Principle 2 — Observation Before Interpretation

Every conclusion begins with observation.

Raw evidence shall always be separated from interpretation.

Assumptions shall never be stored as facts.

---

# 4. Principle 3 — Evidence-Based Intelligence

Meaningful conclusions shall be supported by observable evidence.

Whenever possible, PersonaDNA shall preserve the reasoning behind every
important inference.

---

# 5. Principle 4 — Human-Centric Design

Technology exists to support people.

Every feature should improve human capability, creativity, learning,
communication or understanding.

Human judgement always has priority.

---

# 6. Principle 5 — Continuous Learning

Knowledge is never complete.

PersonaDNA continuously refines understanding while preserving historical
knowledge.

Learning is evolutionary rather than destructive.

---

# 7. Principle 6 — Modularity

Every engine owns one primary responsibility.

Engines communicate through defined interfaces.

Engines remain independently maintainable.

---

# 8. Principle 7 — Reusability

Every capability should be reusable by multiple applications.

Platform intelligence belongs inside PersonaDNA.

Application-specific logic belongs inside applications.

---

# 9. Principle 8 — Explainability

The platform should be capable of explaining how important conclusions
were reached.

Understanding should never become an unexplained black box.

---

# 10. Principle 9 — Knowledge Connectivity

Knowledge has greater value when connected.

Documents, conversations, memories, observations, ideas and people should
be linked whenever meaningful relationships exist.

---

# 11. Principle 10 — Authenticity

PersonaDNA assists individuals in expressing their authentic identity.

Its objective is faithful augmentation rather than imitation.

---

# 12. Principle 11 — Privacy by Design

Personal knowledge belongs to its owner.

The platform shall minimise unnecessary exposure of personal information
and provide clear ownership boundaries.

---

# 13. Principle 12 — Platform Independence

PersonaDNA is an independent platform.

Applications consume PersonaDNA services without changing the platform's
core architecture.

---

# 14. Principle 13 — Long-Term Thinking

Architectural decisions shall prioritise maintainability over short-term
convenience.

Temporary implementation shortcuts shall never become permanent
architecture.

---

# 15. Principle 14 — Simplicity

Simple solutions are preferred over complex ones when both satisfy the
same requirements.

Complexity shall be introduced only when justified.

---

# 16. Principle 15 — Versioned Evolution

The platform evolves through controlled versioning.

Immutable documents remain stable.

Evolutionary documents improve without compromising architectural
integrity.

---

# 17. Compliance

Every engine, API, schema, knowledge model and application integrating
with PersonaDNA shall comply with these Core Principles.

Any deviation requires explicit architectural review.
