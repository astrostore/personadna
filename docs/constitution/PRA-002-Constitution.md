# 1. Purpose

This Constitution defines the permanent governing principles of the PersonaDNA platform.

Every architectural decision, engine, API, knowledge model and application built upon PersonaDNA shall conform to this Constitution.

No implementation shall violate these principles.

---

# 2. Constitutional Mission

PersonaDNA exists to understand human cognitive identity before attempting to assist, generate, recommend or automate.

Understanding shall always take precedence over generation.

---

# 3. Constitutional Principles

## Article 1 — Human First

PersonaDNA exists to serve people.

Technology shall always remain a tool that augments human capability rather than replacing human judgement.

---

## Article 2 — Understanding Before Generation

No intelligence engine shall generate personalized output without first establishing sufficient understanding of the individual.

Observation precedes interpretation.

Interpretation precedes generation.

---

## Article 3 — Evidence Before Conclusion

Every meaningful conclusion shall be supported by observable evidence.

Whenever possible, the platform shall preserve the reasoning that produced an inference.

---

## Article 4 — Identity Is Evolutionary

Human identity is not static.

PersonaDNA shall continuously learn while preserving historical understanding.

Growth is expected.

Contradiction is natural.

Learning never ends.

---

## Article 5 — Knowledge Has Relationships

Knowledge shall never be treated as isolated fragments.

Meaning emerges from relationships between ideas, experiences, memories, people, documents and time.

---

## Article 6 — Explainability

Whenever practical, PersonaDNA shall be capable of explaining why a conclusion, recommendation or generation was produced.

Opaque reasoning should be minimized.

---

## Article 7 — Modularity

Every major capability shall exist as an independent engine with clearly defined ownership and responsibilities.

Engines collaborate.

Engines do not absorb each other's responsibilities.

---

## Article 8 — Reusability

PersonaDNA is a platform.

Applications consume PersonaDNA.

PersonaDNA does not become application-specific.

---

## Article 9 — Privacy

Personal knowledge belongs to the individual.

The platform shall preserve confidentiality, respect ownership and minimize unnecessary exposure of personal information.

---

## Article 10 — Authenticity

PersonaDNA shall assist individuals in expressing their authentic voice.

The objective is faithful augmentation rather than imitation or deception.

---

# 4. Architectural Commitments

PersonaDNA commits to:

- Modular architecture
- Explainable intelligence
- Lifelong learning
- Knowledge preservation
- Transparent reasoning
- Stable interfaces
- Independent engines
- Versioned architecture
- Continuous evolution

---

# 5. Architectural Boundaries

PersonaDNA owns:

- Cognitive understanding
- Persona modelling
- Knowledge modelling
- Memory organisation
- Meaning extraction
- Writing analysis
- Thinking analysis
- Relationship discovery

PersonaDNA does not own:

- User interfaces
- Business workflows
- Domain-specific applications
- Presentation layers

These responsibilities belong to consuming applications.

---

# 6. Constitutional Rules

Every engine shall:

- Have a single primary responsibility.
- Produce deterministic outputs where appropriate.
- Maintain version compatibility.
- Respect architectural boundaries.
- Publish clearly defined interfaces.

---

# 7. Amendment Policy

This Constitution is immutable.

Changes require:

- Architectural review.
- Version increment.
- Approval before adoption.

Minor implementation changes shall never modify constitutional principles.

---

# 8. Constitutional Statement

> PersonaDNA exists to understand people with accuracy, respect and transparency, preserving human identity while augmenting human capability.

This statement shall remain the guiding principle of the platform.
